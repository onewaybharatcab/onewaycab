Bangalore to Coorg: India's Coffee Country by Cab

Coorg is around 250 km from Bangalore and worth every kilometer. We share the best homestay villages to stop at and what to order.

Route & Travel Guides

Coorg (officially Kodagu) is Karnataka's answer to a hill station, minus the crowds most Indian hill towns come with — misty coffee estates, Kodava culture, and a drive down from Bangalore that's genuinely pleasant rather than just a means to an end. Here's how to plan it properly.

The Route via NH275

From Bangalore, NH275 runs through Mysore before turning off toward Kushalnagar and Madikeri, Coorg's main town. The Bangalore-to-Mysore stretch is fast, wide highway; the final leg from Mysore into Coorg's hills is where the drive turns genuinely scenic, winding through coffee and spice plantations as the elevation climbs.

Total drive time runs 5–6 hours, and many travellers break the journey with a stop in Mysore — either on the way down or the way back — to see Mysore Palace, which sits almost exactly on the route.

Best Homestay Villages

Madikeri itself is Coorg's main town and a convenient base, but the real charm is in the smaller estate villages around it — Napoklu, Kabbinakad and Suntikoppa in particular have a strong concentration of family-run coffee-estate homestays that let you walk through working plantations rather than just admiring them from a car window.

Booking a homestay directly on an estate (rather than a standalone hotel in Madikeri town) is generally the better experience if the point of the trip is coffee country rather than just sightseeing.

What to Do and Eat

A guided walk through a coffee estate during harvest season (roughly October–January) is worth prioritising — most homestays can arrange this directly. Abbey Falls and Talakaveri (the source of the Kaveri river) are the two most-visited natural sights, both easy half-day trips from Madikeri.

On the food side, don't miss Coorg's distinct Kodava cuisine — pandi curry (a tangy pork curry) and kadumbuttu (steamed rice dumplings) are specialties you won't easily find outside the region.

💡 Quick Tips

Good to Know Before You Go

☕
Time your visit for the October–January harvest season if a working coffee-estate tour is a priority.
🏡
Book an estate homestay directly rather than a town hotel for a more immersive coffee-country experience.
🐖
Try pandi curry (Kodava pork curry) — it's the region's signature dish and hard to find authentically elsewhere.
🚗
Consider stopping in Mysore on the way — it sits almost directly on the NH275 route to Coorg.
❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Coorg from Bangalore?
+

Coorg's main town, Madikeri, is roughly 250 km from Bangalore, about 5–6 hours by cab via NH275 and Mysore.

Is Mysore worth stopping at on the way to Coorg?
+

Yes — Mysore sits almost directly on the standard Bangalore-to-Coorg route and makes a natural stop to see Mysore Palace on the way down or back.

When is the best time to visit Coorg?
+

October to January is ideal for coffee-harvest season and pleasant weather; the monsoon months (June–September) bring heavy rain but lush, dramatic scenery.
