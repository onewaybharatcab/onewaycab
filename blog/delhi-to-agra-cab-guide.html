Delhi to Agra: Complete Cab Travel Guide for 2026

The Agra highway is iconic. Here's everything — best departure time, road conditions, Taj entry tips and why a cab beats a train for this route.

Route & Travel Guides

Delhi to Agra is India's most popular short cab route for a reason: the Yamuna Expressway is fast, the destination is the Taj Mahal, and the whole round trip is genuinely doable in a single long day if you plan it right. Here's how to actually structure that day, and what to know before you book.

The Yamuna Expressway Route

The Yamuna Expressway is a purpose-built, access-controlled 6-lane highway connecting Greater Noida to Agra, and it's about as smooth a drive as Indian highways get — minimal traffic lights, well-marked exits, and a handful of toll plazas along the 165 km expressway stretch before you join the older road into Agra city itself.

Total drive time from central Delhi runs 3–4 hours depending on how quickly you clear the city and reach the expressway entry point near Greater Noida.

Planning a Same-Day Taj Mahal Trip

If you're doing Agra as a day trip, leave Delhi by 5–6 AM. This gets you to the Taj Mahal for sunrise entry, when the light is best, the heat is manageable, and the crowds are noticeably thinner than mid-morning onward.

A realistic same-day itinerary: Taj Mahal (2 hours), breakfast, Agra Fort (1–1.5 hours), lunch, and a stop at Itmad-ud-Daulah's Tomb or Mehtab Bagh if time allows, before heading back to Delhi by early evening.

What to Know Before You Go

The Taj Mahal is closed to visitors every Friday — check this before locking in your travel date. Only small bags and water bottles are allowed inside the complex; cameras are fine but tripods generally aren't for the general visitor ticket.

Book Taj Mahal entry tickets online in advance where possible — it saves a meaningful amount of queue time, especially in peak winter season (October–March) when Agra sees its highest visitor numbers.

Cab vs Train for a Same-Day Trip

The Gatimaan Express and Shatabdi Express both cover Delhi to Agra in under 2 hours, faster than any road option — but they lock you into fixed departure and return times, which is limiting for a flexible sightseeing day.

A one-way (or same-day round trip) cab gives you full control over timing — leave for sunrise, stay as long as you want at each monument, and head back whenever you're actually done, which matters a lot on a trip built around one specific, time-sensitive sight.

💡 Quick Tips

Good to Know Before You Go

Aim for Taj Mahal gates opening (sunrise) for the best light and smallest crowds of the day.

❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit the Taj Mahal as a day trip from Delhi?
+

Yes — with a 5–6 AM departure, most travellers comfortably fit the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and lunch into a single day and return to Delhi by evening.

How long is the drive from Delhi to Agra?
+

About 3–4 hours by cab via the Yamuna Expressway, depending on Delhi traffic before you reach the expressway entrance.

Is the Taj Mahal open every day?
+

No — it's closed to visitors on Fridays, so it's worth planning your travel date around this if your schedule is fixed.
