Varanasi to Bodhgaya: Pilgrimage Cab Guide

Two of the subcontinent's most sacred cities, 252 km apart. Here's how to plan the drive from the Ganga's ghats to the site of the Buddha's enlightenment.

Route & Travel Guides

Varanasi and Bodhgaya anchor two different, equally profound pilgrimage traditions — one of Hinduism's oldest sacred cities and the site where the Buddha is believed to have attained enlightenment — and the drive between them is a natural add-on for travellers covering North India's spiritual heritage.

The Route

The drive runs through Mughalsarai (now Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Nagar) and Gaya district on NH19 and connecting state highways, mostly flat Gangetic plains terrain. Total drive time is around 5–6 hours, making it a comfortable single-day trip from Varanasi.

What to See in Bodhgaya

The Mahabodhi Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site marking the spot where the Buddha is believed to have attained enlightenment under a Bodhi tree, is the centrepiece of any Bodhgaya visit — the current descendant of the original tree still stands beside the temple.

Bodhgaya is also home to monasteries built by Buddhist communities from across the world — Thai, Tibetan, Japanese, Bhutanese and more — each with distinct architecture, making a walk through the monastery zone worthwhile beyond the main temple itself.

Planning the Trip

Most travellers do this as a day trip from Varanasi, though an overnight in Bodhgaya lets you also see the Mahabodhi Temple at both dawn and dusk, when it's notably quieter than midday.

Combine it naturally with a Varanasi ghat visit and Sarnath (the Buddha's first-sermon site, just outside Varanasi) for a fuller North India Buddhist-Hindu pilgrimage circuit in a single trip.

💡 Quick Tips

Good to Know Before You Go

🛕
Visit the Mahabodhi Temple at dawn or dusk for a quieter, more contemplative experience than midday.
👗
Modest clothing is expected at both the Mahabodhi Temple and the surrounding international monasteries.
🚶
Set aside time to walk through Bodhgaya's monastery zone — the Thai, Tibetan and Japanese temples are each worth a stop.
🗺️
Consider combining this trip with a Sarnath visit near Varanasi for a fuller Buddhist heritage circuit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Bodhgaya from Varanasi?
+

About 252 km, roughly 5–6 hours by cab via NH19 and Gaya district roads.

Can Bodhgaya be visited as a day trip from Varanasi?
+

Yes — most travellers do it as a single long day trip, though an overnight stay lets you see the Mahabodhi Temple at both dawn and dusk when it's quieter.

What is the Mahabodhi Temple?
+

A UNESCO World Heritage Buddhist temple in Bodhgaya marking the spot where the Buddha is believed to have attained enlightenment beneath a Bodhi tree, whose descendant still stands beside the temple today.
